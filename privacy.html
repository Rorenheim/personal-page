Privacy policy

No advertising. No accounts.

Who this is

This privacy policy covers my personal website. I am Paul, and you can reach me at paul@kalaba.me. The site is not used for any commercial activity. I do not sell anything here, and I do not sell, rent, or trade visitor data.

Visiting

The pages, pictures, and video are static files. There is no account, no comment box, no newsletter, and no analytics. I do not set cookies, and the scripts on the page do not store anything about you in the browser.

The machine that serves the files may write a short technical log: the IP address of the connection, the time, the file that was requested, and the browser’s user-agent string. That log exists so the site can be delivered and so misuse can be noticed. I do not use it to identify visitors, and I do not combine it with anything else. I do not keep a separate copy. How long the server holds its own log depends on the host.

Typefaces

These pages load type from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the stylesheet from fonts.googleapis.com and the font files from fonts.gstatic.com. Google therefore receives your IP address and the usual technical details of that request, which can include the address of the page you opened. I do not receive a copy of that data from Google.

The faces are Inter, DM Sans, Space Grotesk, and Doto. Credits and licenses are on the imprint. Google’s own notice is at policies.google.com/privacy. A browser that blocks third-party requests will not make this one; the text then falls back to a standard system face.

Email

If you write to paul@kalaba.me, I receive the message, your address, and the technical headers your mail provider adds. I use that only to read what you sent and to reply. I do not pass it on, and I delete the correspondence when I no longer need it.

Links

LinkedIn and GitHub, where they are linked, are other sites. Opening them leaves this page, and those services apply their own policies. I do not see what you do there.

Your rights

The only personal data I handle myself is what arrives if you email me, plus whatever technical log the host keeps in order to serve the site. You can ask what I hold from a message you sent, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. You can object to my keeping it. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live. Write to paul@kalaba.me. No special form is required.
